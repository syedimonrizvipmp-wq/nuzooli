## 📖 Scholarly Abstract & Epistemological Framework

### English
In mainstream computational linguistics and digital humanities, the Qur’anic corpus has overwhelmingly been processed as a synchronic, static literary monument (*textus receptus* / *Tartīb al-Muṣḥaf*). While liturgically foundational, treating the text as a uniform, non-temporal monolith creates an analytical blind spot: it dissociates divine discourse from its **23-year dynamic historical dialogue (610–632 CE)**, obscuring the pedagogical progression of revelation (*Tadrīj al-Tanzīl*), situational socio-political interventions (*Asbāb al-Nuzūl*), and stratified legislative maturation (*Tadrīj al-Aḥkām*).

**Project Nuzooli** resolves this epistemological deficit. As an open-access, peer-reviewed-grade Digital Humanities laboratory, it operationalizes the diachronic sequence of revelation through quantitative spatial-temporal modeling, interactive timeline synchronization, and reproducible FAIR datasets. By bridging classical Islamic exegetical authority (*`Ulūm al-Qur’ān*) with computational corpus cartography, the platform provides researchers, philologists, and theologians with an empirical lens to interrogate the living dialectic of the prophetic mission.

### Pillar 1: The Mathematical Delta Displacement Metric ($\Delta$)
The structural divergence between the chronological descent order (*Tartīb al-Nuzūl*) and the canonical Ottoman codex (*Tartīb al-Muṣḥaf*) is formalized via the **Delta Displacement Formula**:

$$\Delta_s = |N_{\text{nuzul}}(s) - M_{\text{mushaf}}(s)|$$

Where:
- $N_{\text{nuzul}}(s) \in [1, 114]$ denotes the chronological revelation index of Surah $s$.
- $M_{\text{mushaf}}(s) \in [1, 114]$ denotes the canonical recension position of Surah $s$.
- $\Delta_s \in [0, 113]$ quantifies the magnitude of structural transposition.

*Hermeneutical Significance:* High-$\Delta$ surahs illuminate the profound pedagogical distance between the dawn of revelation (e.g., **Sūrat al-ʿAlaq**, $N=1, M=96 \implies \mathbf{\Delta = 95}$) and its liturgical canonization, providing empirical data for structuralist textual analysis.

### Pillar 2: Gradual Legal Evolution Engine (*Tadrīj al-Aḥkām*)
Islamic jurisprudence (*Fiqh*) establishes that statutory ordinances were pedagogical interventions cultivated across time rather than instantaneous mandates. Project Nuzooli tracks **5 distinct legal trajectories across 15+ granular milestones**:
1. **Prohibition of Intoxicants (تحریم الخمر):** 4 progressive stages (Nahl 67 $\rightarrow$ Baqarah 219 $\rightarrow$ Nisa 43 $\rightarrow$ Maidah 90–91).
2. **Armed Defense & Just Warfare (تشريع الجهاد):** 3 ethical transitions (Hajj 39 $\rightarrow$ Baqarah 190 $\rightarrow$ Tawbah 36).
3. **Orientation of the Sacred Qiblah (تحویل القبلة):** 2 cosmological stages (Bayt al-Maqdis $\rightarrow$ Masjid al-Haram, Baqarah 142–144).
4. **The Mandate of Fasting (تشريع الصيام):** 3 evolutionary ascents ('Ashura voluntary $\rightarrow$ Baqarah 183 $\rightarrow$ Baqarah 187 nocturnal concessions).
5. **Zakat & Wealth Purification (الزكاة والإنفاق):** 3 economic strata (Meccan voluntary altruism $\rightarrow$ Rum 39 $\rightarrow$ Tawbah 60/103 institutional treasury).

### Pillar 3: Crisis Intervention & Situational Feedback (*Asbāb al-Nuzūl*)
The platform models **9 decisive prophetic crisis junctures** (The Cessation of Revelation / *Fatrat al-Waḥy*, the economic siege of *Shiʿb Abī Ṭālib*, the bereavement of *ʿĀm al-Ḥuzn*, the Battle of Uḥud, the slander crisis / *Ḥadīth al-Ifk*, and the Treaty of *Ḥudaybiyyah*) as dynamic socio-historical feedback loops.

### Pillar 4: Sacred Revelation Topography & Geospatial Cartography
A curated geospatial layer connects revelation events to **16 GPS-indexed physical coordinates** across Western Arabia (The Cave of Ḥirāʾ, Mount Thawr, Dār al-Arqam, the valley of Minā, Qubāʾ, the mosque of the two Qiblahs, and the battlefield of Badr), grounding transcendental scripture in tangible geography.

## 💾 FAIR Data Downloads & Reproducibility (محققین کے لیے خام مواد)

In compliance with the **FAIR Data Principles** (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable), all core datasets are available without authentication:

### 1. Available Open-Access Artifacts
- **[`nuzooli-chronology-matrix.csv`](https://nuzooli.vercel.app):** RFC 4180 CSV containing all 114 Surahs, Canonical Rank ($M$), Chronological Rank ($N$), Delta Displacement ($\Delta$), Revelation Epoch, Classification, and Verse counts.
- **[`nuzooli-chronology-matrix.json`](https://nuzooli.vercel.app):** Complete JSON-LD compliant machine-readable chronological matrix with scholarly provenance metadata.
- **[`gradual-legislation-tracks.json`](https://nuzooli.vercel.app):** The 5 legal evolution tracks, 15 historical stages, scriptural citations, and primary exegetical citations.

### 2. Computational Access via Python / Pandas
International researchers can ingest and analyze the dataset with 2 lines of code:

```python
import pandas as pd

# Load the FAIR Chronology Matrix into a Pandas DataFrame
url = "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/syedimonrizvipmp-wq/nuzooli/main/src/data/surahs.ts" # or exported CSV
df = pd.read_csv("nuzooli-chronology-matrix.csv")

# Filter high-displacement Meccan surahs (Delta > 50)
high_delta_meccan = df[(df['Delta_Displacement'] > 50) & (df['Classification'] == 'meccan')]
print(high_delta_meccan[['Surah_Name_English', 'Chronological_Nuzul_Rank', 'Canonical_Mushaf_Rank', 'Delta_Displacement']])
```

## ⚖️ Methodological Transparency, AI Governance & Scholarly Disclaimer

### English
**1. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Computational Architecture:**  
Project Nuzooli utilizes advanced natural language processing (NLP) and large language models (LLMs) strictly as computational research instruments—accelerating corpus indexing, structural cross-referencing, and multi-perspective chronological synthesis. All algorithmic outputs, thematic taxonomies, and diachronic alignments are critically audited, cross-verified, and contextualized under traditional philological and seminary-trained (*Hawzah / Dars-e-Nizami*) oversight.

**2. Non-Prescriptive & Historical-Analytical Scope:**  
This platform is an academic research instrument dedicated to diachronic text exploration (*Tarteeb-e-Nuzul*) and comparative legal pedagogy (*Tadrij al-Ahkam*). It does not constitute a canonical legislative edict (*fatwa*), nor does it seek to alter the universally consecrated canonical sequence of the *Uthmanic Mushaf*. All chronological sequences are investigative frameworks grounded in classical historical reports and modern hermeneutical models.

**3. Primary Source Provenance & Continuous Peer Review:**  
While data models strive for the highest philological fidelity against canonical authorities—principally Ayatollah Muhammad Hadi Ma'rifat's *Al-Tamhid fi 'Ulum al-Qur'an*, Allamah Muhammad Husayn Tabataba'i's *Tafsir al-Mizan*, and classical *Asbab al-Nuzul* literature—this repository operates under open-science principles. It is subject to continuous scholarly peer review, critical refinement, and academic versioning. Users and researchers are encouraged to verify textual contexts directly against cited classical editions.

**4. Complete Limitation of Theological & Legal Liability:**  
All data, analytical charts, and exportable datasets are made accessible strictly for non-commercial academic inquiry, scientific research, and pedagogical study. The principal investigator, developers, and affiliated contributors disclaim any religious, sectarian, or legal liability for any third-party interpretations, typographical oversights, or derivative uses of this computational framework.
